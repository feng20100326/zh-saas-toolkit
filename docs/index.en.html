1. Why one stack instead of four tools

Hosting + site builder + email tool + CRM + automation = five logins, five bills, and your customer data scattered across all of them. For a small team the real cost is not the monthly fee — it is the fragmentation of attention.

2. Selection criteria (in order)

  1. One stack: site + email + automation + orders + membership in one dashboard.
  2. Starts under $20/month, scales without a re-buy.
  3. Own your data: CSV export, custom domain, no hostage lock-in.
  4. Chinese-language usability.
  5. Referral attribution: does a link stay attributed to you for life, or does it die with a 30-day cookie?

4. Three common profiles

  • Cross-border store + email → systeme.io (Startup $17/mo): funnels, email campaigns, automations, orders, one-click upsells in one place.
  • Ecommerce with cart-recovery / repeat purchase → systeme.io Unlimited or Omnisend.
  • Booking-heavy service business → Cal.com for scheduling + systeme.io for reminders, delivery and repeat purchase.
